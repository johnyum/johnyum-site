Rules

One number34px, bottom-left, where the name used to be. The unit is small and muted.
One line of chart, or noneA rate gets a hairline of history. A stage gets a strip. A temp gets two rows. Nothing gets a dial.
Ink onlyThe only color in a widget is the terracotta dot marking now. Crayons stay in the drawings.
The name staysStill at the bottom, still 13px. The tile reads as the same tile, now with something to say.
Nothing to tap insideNo pills, no chevrons, no dots. The tile is the button; the Space holds the controls.
1×1s untouchedDrawing and name. The wall stays calm because most of it is quiet.
