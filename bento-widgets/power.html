DialThe AC gauge. A 270° tick ring, lit up to the value, the number sitting inside. For anything on a scale you keep re-checking: the rate, the pull temp.
HeadlineThe $924.18 card. One big number, a delta pill in lime, a line of specifics under it. For the thing that changed since last time.
ScenesTwo tinted sub-cards plus a footer row. When a group has two distinct jobs (offer math vs. rate watch) instead of one.
BarsThe Activity chart. Ink bars, one in terracotta for today. Wall-level, not group-level: how much you've been talking.
ListIcon, name, two-line spec, chevron. The drawings do the work the device icons do in the reference.
And nothing on the 1×1sSmall tiles stay the drawing and the name. The widgets earn attention by being rare.

Rules of the widget

The drawing becomes the markShrinks to 28px, moves to the top-left, keeps its wobble. Never a system glyph next to a playful drawing.
One number per tile34px semibold, tight tracking, unit in muted 14px. A second number is allowed only at 26px and only on the hero.
Lime means changeThe one loud color is reserved for the delta pill and the greeting strip. Crayon colors only light up dials and scene tints.
Pills, not buttonsBubble-grey pills for actions, ghost pills for counts, ink pill for the single primary. Same capsule the model picker uses.
Spoken to youLabels read like the insight engine already writes them: "rate you keep re-checking", "Roma this week". Lowercase, complete thoughts.
Tap still drills inThe whole tile opens the Space. Pills inside are the only secondary targets, and there's never more than one.
