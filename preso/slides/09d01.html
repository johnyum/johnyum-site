Guests hated surprise fees at checkout, including a service fee of about 14% on top of cleaning, and hosts still had to cover cleaning. So we moved to one price shown upfront that removes the sticker shock, and hosts set a price that covers their costs.

For hosts, the whole service fee now comes out of their payout: about 15.5%, up from 3%. So hosts had to raise their prices to keep their earnings the same.

Guests would see no difference.

Hosts were about to be very angry.
