Chat to Dates Motion

Seven fixes for the jitter when the Ask chat folds into the dates UI. Each pair loops on a four second cycle: the box drops on a tap, holds, then returns. Left is what the phone does now, right is the proposal.

1. One world, not two crossfading

Today the thread fades out while a separate panel fades in, and the box height changes at the same time. Instead, make the Signpost and tools the top block of the thread and let the shrinking box do the revealing. The thread simply slides out the bottom.

The dates block is already the first thing in the thread. Shrinking the box hides the rest.

2. Only the box animates

Content that repositions while its container moves reads as jitter. Freeze the thread during the drop and let the box clip it. The three timed scroll passes were the source of the wobble.

Now: content re-scrolls mid-drop
Oct 14–16 · 3 nights

Scroll passes at 0, 120 and 350 ms fight the height spring.

Proposed: thread frozen, box clips
Oct 14–16 · 3 nights

One spring. Nothing inside the box moves relative to the box.

3. Stagger the small moves after the big one

Land the box first. Then fold the field into the orb. Then bring in the tools. Three beats of 80 to 120 ms feel deliberate. All at once feels like a glitch. Reverse the order on the way back.

4. Give the tools a shared origin

Four circles appearing in place have no story. Let them rise from the orb's position, or scale from the Signpost's centre, with a 30 ms stagger. Same idea as the sparkle cascade on the brief pills.

5. Anchor the Signpost to the pull tab

The text should sit a fixed 16 pt under the tab at every height. Then the tab and the text become a stable handle for the state, and the words never shift when the box does.

7. One state, not five flags

Right now the dates state is inferred from hasSelection, sheet, h, pinned and typing agreeing. In between frames they disagree. Drive it from one enum on the model and let every view read that.

Now: five flags, many in-between frames
hasSelectionsheet == .askh < mediumpinnedtyping

Any frame where one lags shows a mixed layout.

Proposed: askMode
.threadScrolling chat, full field.
.datesSignpost, tools, orb. Locked.
.typingField open over the keys.

Set once on tap or clear. Views never guess.
