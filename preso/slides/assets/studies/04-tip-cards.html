Where I'd start

Build 1 and 3 as a single card: the strip of nights, one control, Apply that animates the calendar. That's the moment the chat stops being a chat and becomes the tool. Then 6, because a rule is the natural end of a tip. 2, 4 and 5 are layers on top once that card exists. Every one of these fits the fixed-height card block already in the app, so none of it fights the sheet's geometry.
