On the market

Bay Area Eichler inventory runs about twenty to thirty-five houses at any moment, which is why this fits on one screen. Redfin and Zillow can't be read programmatically from here, so this is the union of four specialist IDX feeds. Treat it as a lead list, not a system of record — status and days on market disagree between sites, and the deep links on the Tracts tab are what you should actually run each morning.

Can you build on it?

Single-storey overlays are drawn to parcel lines, not tract lines, and often skip corner lots. Use this to know which conversation to have, then pull the parcel's own zoning before you write.

Where prices sit

Most Eichler-segmented pricing comes from brokerages that specialise in Eichlers and have an interest in strong numbers. The San Mateo Highlands row is the one neutral, MLS-derived cross-check, and it broadly agrees with them. Marin and East Bay rows are active list prices, not closed sales, so read them 15% optimistic.

Two numbers worth carrying: Eichlers close at about 109–110% of list against roughly 102% for the broader Silicon Valley market, and turnkey trades ~36% above fixer on a price-per-foot basis in San Jose, a spread of roughly $650K. A badly remodeled Eichler — dropped ceilings, boxed beams, vinyl windows — can sell below an untouched original.
